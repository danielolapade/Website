Research Project

2024

Interactive STEM Exhibit on Aviation Fuel
Aviation fuel has a past, but what about its future? The Royal Aviation Museum of Western Canada (RAMWC) challenged my team to design a STEM exhibit on aviation fuel for visitors aged 7+.

Our goal? Uncover the history of aviation fuel, explore the challenges of greener alternatives, and inspire the next generation of problem-solvers through interactive learning.

Proposed Solution: A three-part exhibit featuring a historical timeline of aviation fuel, energy density station, and a carbon emissions station to explore sustainability challenges.
